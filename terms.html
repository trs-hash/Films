Autoposter101 is a private automation tool operated by Taras Maksymiak. It takes short videos prepared on the operator's own server and publishes them to a single authorised TikTok account. These terms describe how the tool may be used.

Who may use it

The tool is for the operator's personal use only. It is not distributed, sold, or made available to other people, and it does not accept registrations. Only the account holder who authorises it through TikTok's login screen can use it, and only for their own account.

What the tool does

  • Uploads video files to the authorised TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API.
  • Sets a caption and publishes the video, or leaves it as a draft.
  • Reads basic account information to confirm the correct account is connected.

It does not read, download, or repost other people's content, does not follow or message anyone, and does not interact with the TikTok feed in any way.

The operator is solely responsible for every video published through this tool, including holding the necessary rights to the footage and music used. All published content must comply with TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service. Any video found to breach them will be removed.

Ending access

Authorisation can be withdrawn at any time from TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services. Withdrawal takes effect immediately and stops all publishing. TikTok may also suspend the app's API access at its own discretion.
