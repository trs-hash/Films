Autoposter101

A private tool that publishes the operator's own short videos to their own TikTok account — one video at a time, always confirmed by a person.

Autoposter101 runs on its operator's own home server. It takes short vertical videos that the operator has already made, and helps publish them to the operator's own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API. Nothing is posted automatically: every video passes through a confirmation screen where a human sets the caption, chooses who can see it, and presses Publish.

What it does

  • Shows the connected account's nickname and avatar so the operator can confirm the right account.
  • Presents a confirmation screen with caption, privacy level, comment / Duet / Stitch settings and commercial-content disclosure.
  • Uploads the video and creates the post only after the operator presses Publish, then tracks the publish status.

What it does not do

  • It does not read, download, or repost anyone else's content.
  • It does not browse, like, follow, comment, or message on TikTok.
  • It is not offered to other people and has no sign-up — it serves a single operator's own account.
